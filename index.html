About Me

Understanding people, one brushstroke at a time.

I'm a psychology student fascinated by the inner landscape of the human mind: how we think, why we feel, and what quietly shapes the way we behave. In every lecture and every book, I look for the stories behind human experience.

Alongside psychology, painting is my second language. Colour, light and form let me express what words sometimes cannot, and they teach me to observe people and the world with patience and tenderness. For me, science and art are two ways of asking the same question: what does it mean to be human?
